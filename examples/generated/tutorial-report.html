You can now do the first-level profit breakdown and the per-store revenue calculation unaided, and you stopped needing hints from the third rep onward. What has not settled yet: after listing branches you do not say which one you would look at first, or why.

The three things that matter most

If you read nothing else, read this.

Strongest work

You fix the unit of the answer before you calculate

On the first attempt you produced 117,000 and could not say what it meant. Once prompted, you wrote down “pounds per store per month”, worked backwards to the inputs, and got both the number and the unit right. That habit will prevent most calculation errors you would otherwise make.

Biggest gap

You can produce branches, but never say which one comes first

In three of four structuring reps you stopped at listing branches. A structure exists to decide what to investigate next; one that cannot be ordered cannot move a real case forward.

Evidence: View T02 View T04

Train this next

Name the first branch and the reason, every time

Next session, five structuring reps under one rule: after the branches, add “I would start with X, because Y”. Delete any branch you cannot justify — if you cannot justify it, it usually did not belong there.

T02YouStructureStructure
I'd look at market, customer and competition.
Critical errorStructurepost-session comment

This is a general framework, not a structure for this case

None of market, customer or competition answers “why did profit fall”. The client asked about profit, and profit has an explicit formula — that formula is where the structure should start.

For any problem, first ask how the metric the client cares about is computed, then break down that formula.

T03CoachStructureHint
What is the client actually trying to fix, and what is profit computed from? Use those two questions to rebuild the first level.
Hint givenTeachingpost-session comment

You were not handed branches here, only two questions to rebuild from. Structuring has to be built rather than received, so a worked answer would have taught nothing.

T04YouStructureSecond attemptKey moment
The client wants to restore profit, so I would start with profit equals revenue minus cost. I would split revenue into price and volume, then split volume into footfall and conversion.
Needs workPrioritisationpost-session comment

The branches are listed but not ordered. Revenue up 4% with profit down 12% already points at cost or mix, which is enough to choose a starting point out loud.

Improved after the hintStructurepost-session comment

Starting from profit = revenue − cost and splitting revenue into price and volume is correct, and you did it on the second attempt without further help.

The cost side is still empty though. Store profit fell 12% while revenue rose, which points at cost — that branch deserved priority.

If you remember three things

  1. Build the structure from the formula behind the metric the client asked about, not from a framework you memorised.
  2. A structure is not finished until the branches are ordered — an unordered structure cannot move the analysis.
  3. Write down the unit of the answer first and the calculation path usually appears.
